The network is never told what the score is where there is no data

The score-matching loss is an expectation under pdata, so empty regions contribute nothing to it. The model below is really fit — random Fourier features, trained by denoising score matching in closed form on the sample cloud you see. Nothing about its behaviour away from the data is staged.

Where the training target comes from

You never need to know ∇log pσ to train. For each clean point x you draw a perturbation and regress on ∇x̃ log pσ(x̃ | x) = (x − x̃)/σ², which points straight back along the perturbation you just applied. It is exact and free, wherever x̃ happens to land — which is exactly why cranking σ up costs you nothing in supervision.

Dashed line: the perturbation σz that was applied. Solid arrow: the regression target, pointing back to the clean point. Averaged over many draws, this regression converges to ∇log pσ(x̃) — the marginal score, not the conditional one.

What to try

  1. Start at σ = 0.05. The orange cloud sits right on top of the data — supervised area is a few percent. Everywhere else the arrows are arbitrary, and the error map is red across the entire region your chains start in. Watch the purple dots drift sideways, get pulled into corners, and pool in places with no data at all. They are being steered by a function that was never fit to anything.
  2. Drag σ up to ~1.0. The training cloud inflates until it covers the box, the error map goes green nearly everywhere, and the arrows all point coherently toward the modes. Every chain now finds the data. But look at the effective mode width — you are sampling a blurred distribution.
  3. Turn on "show the true score field" at small σ and compare. The true field is perfectly well behaved in the empty regions; it is only the estimate that is broken there, because the loss put zero weight on those regions.
  4. Run annealed Langevin. The model is refit at each rung of the σ ladder. The wide rungs give a trustworthy field far from the data and get every chain into the right neighbourhood; by the time σ is small again, no chain is ever evaluated in an unsupervised region, so the broken arrows out in the corners no longer matter.
